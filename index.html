CASE STUDY

Indian E-Commerce Sales & Insights Analytics

Overview

Designed a structured database and analytical report to evaluate order trends, geographical revenue distribution, and payment patterns across top Indian metropolitan cities.

Problem & Approach

Raw multi-city sales entries needed normalization and dynamic aggregation to allow stakeholders to filter performance by location, month, and fulfillment mode.

Key Tools

  • SQL (Schema design, analytical query processing)
  • Power BI (DAX metrics, geographic maps)
  • Advanced Excel (Data cleaning, validation)

Takeaway

Interactive reporting reduces manual slide creation and gives teams immediate clarity on city-level operational performance.

CASE STUDY

Traffic Mortality Hotspot Analysis

Overview

A geospatial visual mapping tool focused on plotting location coordinates of traffic incidents to assist urban planners in identifying high-density risk areas.

Problem & Approach

Tabular crash logs make identifying spatial density patterns difficult. By converting coordinate metrics to dynamic density heatmaps, spatial risks become visually intuitive.

Key Tools

  • Leaflet.js (Map rendering & dynamic layering)
  • OpenStreetMap & CARTO Tiles
  • JavaScript (Geospatial parsing)

Takeaway

Geospatial analysis allows safety interventions to be targeted precisely where incidents recur most frequently.
